What we offer

Everything a student needs
to thrive.

Science-backed tools delivered through a beautifully simple app. Available free, anytime, anywhere.

🧘

Guided Meditation

Culturally adapted sessions for exam stress, social anxiety, sleep, and burnout. 3-minute quick resets to 20-minute deep practices.

📓

Smart Journaling

AI-prompted journaling that reflects your mood patterns, highlights growth, and offers evidence-based CBT reframing exercises.

🧠

CBT Self-Help Tools

Cognitive behavioral therapy exercises designed for student-specific stress: thought challenging, worry time, and behavioral activation.

🤖

AI Wellness Coach

Powered by advanced NLP, your personal AI coach is available 24/7. Empathetic, non-judgmental, trained on clinical best practices.

📊

Mood Tracking

Track how you feel daily and discover patterns over time. Insights connect mood to sleep, study load, and social activity.

🌏

Culturally Localized

Adapted for India, Japan, and the UK. Native language support, local cultural references, and region-specific mental health framing.
