FAQ

Common questions

Is the free tier genuinely useful, or just a trial?
The free tier is a permanent, full-featured option. Core meditation, journaling, AI chat, and mood tracking are always free. Mental health support should not be gated behind payment.
How does ThriveSpace compare to Calm or Headspace on price?
ThriveSpace Premium is $7.99 per month versus Calm at $14.99 and Headspace at $12.99. We are also the only one built specifically for students, with cultural localization and exam-focused content.
Is my data private and secure?
Yes. Journals and mood logs are end-to-end encrypted. We comply with GDPR in the UK, Japan's APPI, and India's DPDP Act. You can download or delete all your data at any time.
Can ThriveSpace replace therapy?
No, and we are transparent about this. ThriveSpace is designed as a supplement to professional care, not a replacement. We actively encourage users to seek therapy and route crisis situations to local emergency resources.
