FAQ

Common questions

Is the free tier genuinely useful, or just a trial?
The free tier is a permanent, full-featured option. We believe mental health support shouldn't be gated behind payment. Core meditation, journaling, AI chat, and mood tracking are always free.
How does ThriveSpace compare to Calm or Headspace on price?
ThriveSpace Premium is $7.99/month vs Calm at $14.99 and Headspace at $12.99 — and we're the only one built specifically for students with cultural localization and exam-focused content.
Is my data private and secure?
Yes. Journals and mood logs are end-to-end encrypted. We comply with GDPR (UK), Japan's APPI, and India's DPDP Act. You can download or delete all your data at any time.
Can ThriveSpace replace therapy?
No — and we're transparent about this. ThriveSpace is designed as a supplement to professional care, not a replacement. We actively encourage users to seek therapy and route crisis situations to local emergency resources.
