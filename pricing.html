FAQ

Common questions

Is the free tier genuinely useful or just a trial? +
The free tier is a permanent, full-featured option. We believe mental health support should not be gated behind payment. Core meditation, journaling, AI chat, and mood tracking are always free with no expiration.
How does ThriveSpace compare on price to competitors? +
ThriveSpace Premium is $7.99 per month compared to Calm at $14.99 and Headspace at $12.99. We are the only one built specifically for students with cultural localization and exam-focused content at this price point.
Is my data private and secure? +
Yes. Journals and mood logs are end-to-end encrypted. We comply with GDPR in the UK, Japan's APPI, and India's DPDP Act. You can download or delete all your data at any time through the app settings.
Can ThriveSpace replace therapy? +
No, and we are fully transparent about this. ThriveSpace is designed as a supplement to professional care, not a replacement. We actively encourage users to seek therapy and immediately route crisis situations to local emergency resources.
