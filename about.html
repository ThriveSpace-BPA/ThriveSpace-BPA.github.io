About ThriveSpace

Mental wellness is a right, not a luxury

ThriveSpace was built on a simple belief: every student, regardless of where they live, what language they speak, or what they can afford, deserves access to mental health support.

We combine AI, evidence-based psychology, and deep cultural sensitivity to reach students that traditional mental healthcare has failed to serve.

Our Mission

Why we exist

74% of young people report worsened mental health since the pandemic. In India, Japan, and the UK, the barriers are different: stigma, wait times, cost. But the pain is the same.


ThriveSpace exists to close that gap. Not to replace therapists, but to fill the silence between "I'm struggling" and "I got help."

"Mental wellness is not a luxury. It is what allows students to learn, grow, and become the people the world needs them to be."
Our Values

What drives every decision

🌍
Accessibility First
Core features are free, always. No student should be locked out of mental health support because of price.
🛡️
Safety Without Compromise
Crisis detection, emergency hotlines, and clinical review of all content. We take safety more seriously than engagement.
🌏
Cultural Respect
We do not export Western mental health frameworks. We adapt to local values, language, and cultural context.
🔒
Privacy by Design
End-to-end encryption. GDPR, APPI, and DPDP compliant. Your data is yours, export or delete anytime.
🤝
Supplement, Not Replace
We partner with therapists and counselors. ThriveSpace bridges the gap; it does not try to close the door on professional care.
📊
Data-Driven Impact
Every feature is measured. If something does not help students, we change it. Mission and metrics reinforce each other.

Marketing Timeline

12-Month Roadmap

Q1 · Months 1 to 3
Foundation Phase
Localize app for India, Japan, and the UK. Beta test with 3,000 students. Produce marketing materials, social assets, and QR codes for campus distribution.
Q2 · Months 4 to 6
Launch Phase
Social campaigns on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. Campus launch events with free 2-week premium trials. Target: 25,000 downloads.
Q3 · Months 7 to 9
Expansion Phase
Analyze user data, scale region-specific content. Increase premium adoption to 3.5% of active users. Partner with school counseling centers for referrals.
Q4 · Months 10 to 12
Optimization Phase
Refine pricing, onboarding, and retention. Re-engage inactive users. Publish annual progress report. Target: 60,000 total downloads and 5% conversion.
