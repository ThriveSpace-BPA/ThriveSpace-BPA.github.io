Feature 03 · AI Chat

AI Mental Health Chat

Available 24/7, the chat uses CBT-based techniques to help students work through anxiety, stress, and difficult emotions. Designed to be honest about its own limits and to point toward professional care when needed.

  • CBT and mindfulness techniques in every conversation
  • Crisis detection instantly surfaces local emergency numbers
  • Never replaces therapy, actively encourages professional support
  • Available in English, Hindi, and Japanese
  • Conversation history for tracking thoughts over time
🛡️

Safety built in: The AI detects crisis keywords and immediately provides local emergency hotlines. It is a supplement to professional care, never a replacement.

Feature 04 · Mood Tracking

Mood Tracking & Progress

Daily check-ins take under 30 seconds. Over time, ThriveSpace surfaces patterns, suggests better timing for hard tasks, and shows you how far you've come.

  • 30-second emoji-based daily check-ins
  • Weekly and monthly trend visualization
  • Pattern detection: "You tend to feel anxious on Sunday nights"
  • Opt-in mood reports that can be shared with a counselor
  • Streak rewards to build healthy check-in habits
