💬 Feature 03

AI Mental Health Chat

Always-available CBT-based conversations to work through anxiety, stress, and difficult emotions. Trained to be safe, empathetic, and honest about its limits.

  • CBT and mindfulness techniques in every conversation
  • Crisis detection instantly surfaces local emergency numbers
  • Never replaces therapy; encourages professional support
  • Multilingual: English, Hindi, Japanese
  • Conversation history for tracking thoughts over time
🛡️

Safety first: ThriveSpace AI detects crisis keywords and immediately provides local emergency hotlines. It is a supplement, never a replacement, for professional care.
