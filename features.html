Feature 03

AI Mental Health Chat

Always-available CBT-based conversations to work through anxiety, stress, and difficult emotions. Trained to be safe, empathetic, and honest about its limitations as a supplement to professional care.

  • CBT and mindfulness techniques embedded in every conversation
  • Crisis detection instantly surfaces local emergency numbers
  • Never replaces therapy and actively encourages professional support
  • Multilingual support in English, Hindi, and Japanese
  • Conversation history for tracking thoughts over time
🛡

Safety First: ThriveSpace AI detects crisis keywords and immediately provides local emergency hotlines. It is a supplement, never a replacement, for professional care.

Feature 04

Mood Tracking and Progress

Daily check-ins take under 30 seconds. Over time ThriveSpace surfaces patterns, suggests interventions, and shows you how far you have come on your wellness journey.

  • 30-second daily mood check-ins with emoji-based logging
  • Weekly and monthly trend visualization in clear charts
  • Pattern detection: "You tend to feel anxious on Sunday nights"
  • Shareable mood reports for counselors via opt-in only
  • Streak rewards to build healthy check-in habits
