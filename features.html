CBT Tools

Rewire unhelpful thinking.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy is one of the most evidence-backed approaches for anxiety and depression. ThriveSpace makes it accessible, interactive, and student-friendly.

  • * Thought records to challenge negative thinking
  • * Worry time scheduling to contain anxious thoughts
  • * Behavioral activation for low mood and depression
  • * Fear ladders for social anxiety and avoidance
  • * Psychoeducation modules on stress and emotions

Localization

Built for your culture.

Mental health is deeply cultural. ThriveSpace adapts language, imagery, metaphors, payment methods, and content priorities for each region.

  • * Full native language support (Hindi, Japanese, English)
  • * Region-specific content priorities and stress topics
  • * Local payment methods (UPI, Konbini, UK bank transfer)
  • * Culturally adapted visual themes per region
  • * GDPR, APPI, and India DPDP Act compliant
India Key Adaptations
JEE/NEET exam stress focus. Android-first UI, Hindi and English support, WhatsApp sharing, UPI payments. Community framing aligned with family-centered values.
Japan Key Adaptations
Anime-inspired visual themes. Emphasis on harmony and mindful reflection. Discreet UI. LINE social integration, Konbini payments. Addressing school-related isolation.
UK Key Adaptations
NHS-complementary positioning. Signposting to Samaritans and MIND. CBT tools aligned with NHS IAPT approach. University student focus, British English throughout.
